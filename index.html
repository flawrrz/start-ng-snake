Got a game?
Put it on start.ng.

Any static browser game can live here. Players arrive with their start.ng character, scores and friends already in hand.

  1. 01

    Build it

    Any static browser game works: HTML, JavaScript, three.js or Phaser.

  2. 02

    Push it to GitHub

    A public repo with a ready-to-play index.html at the top or in dist/.

  3. 03

    Publish it

    One API call with your repo link, no key needed. Or hand your AI the docs.

  4. 04

    Approve it once

    It goes live at yourgame.start.ng, with a leaderboard if you want one.

The story

About start.ng

start.ng is Nigeria’s home for browser games, built by the HNG community. Every game is free to play in your browser with no download, and many are made in Naija: danfo rush hour, jollof catching, Lagos markets and more.

We’re also building a home for web games from across Africa. If you make games anywhere on the continent, you can put yours on start.ng for free.

Questions

What is start.ng?

A home for free browser games made by the HNG community. Each game has its own address, like yourgame.start.ng, and you play it right in your browser.

Is it free?

Yes. Every game on start.ng is free to play in your browser, with nothing to download.

Are there Nigerian or African web games?

Yes. Many games here are Naija-themed, from danfo rush hour to jollof and Lagos markets, and we welcome games from across Africa.

Do I need an account to play?

No. Sign in to get your start.ng character, keep your scores on the leaderboards and join the daily tournament.

How do I put my game on start.ng?

Push your browser game to a public GitHub repo, publish it with one API call and approve it once. It goes live at yourgame.start.ng.
